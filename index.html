About

I am a doctoral researcher at the Vellore Institute of Technology, working in High Performance Computing. My research sits where applied cryptography meets GPU architecture: taking schemes that are secure but costly, and restructuring them so thousands of GPU threads can process them in parallel.

Before the PhD, I completed an M.Tech in Embedded Systems at VIT and a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication at Shivaji University, Kolhapur. That hardware background, from 8051 and ARM Cortex-M3 to VHDL on FPGAs, shapes how I think about memory, latency and throughput on the GPU.

GPU computing

CUDA C/C++ kernels, batch processing and memory-aware optimisation for throughput.

Post-quantum cryptography

Lattice-based schemes such as ML-KEM, accelerated for real-world key exchange.

Lightweight cryptography

Efficient primitives and searchable encryption for constrained and large-scale systems.

Embedded systems

RTOS, ARM Cortex-M3, VHDL and IoT devices connected to the cloud.
